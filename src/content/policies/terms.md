These Terms govern every engagement with The Standard Solutions ("we", "us", "our"). They apply alongside your individual engagement letter. Where the two conflict, the engagement letter prevails.

## 1. Scope of services

We provide the following, working primarily through client-granted access to cloud accounting software and secure cloud storage:

- **Bookkeeping** — transaction processing, bank and credit card reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, and general ledger maintenance on platforms including Xero, QuickBooks Online and Zoho Books.
- **Accounting and reporting** — periodic management accounts, including profit and loss statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements.
- **Payroll** — payroll processing, payslip preparation and related filings where agreed.
- **Taxation** — Pakistan: preparation and filing of business and individual returns. United Kingdom: VAT returns and corporation tax for limited companies. United Arab Emirates: VAT and corporate tax returns. Australia: bookkeeping and advisory only — Business Activity Statements and income tax returns are lodged by your registered tax or BAS agent. United States: preparation and filing support.
- **Management review services** — transaction vouching and tracing, internal control checks and verification of account balances. The deliverable is a management report or checklist setting out findings, discrepancies and recommendations.
- **Advisory** — fractional CFO and management advisory work as scoped in the engagement letter.

## 2. What we do not provide

- We do not perform statutory audits in any jurisdiction. We issue no audit opinion and do not sign or stamp financial statements in an audit capacity.
- We do not provide legal advice, certified financial planning or investment advice. Commentary we give is professional opinion for information only and should not be your sole basis for a financial decision.
- We do not represent you before tax authorities, regulators or in legal proceedings unless your engagement letter expressly says otherwise.
- We do not provide services outside the scope recorded in your engagement letter. Additional work is quoted and agreed separately before it begins.

Before and during an engagement we carry out client due diligence proportionate to the risk of the work. This includes verifying the identity and ownership of the client entity, understanding the source of funds where relevant, and screening against applicable sanctions and adverse media. We may request identification documents, proof of address and ownership records at any point.

We reserve the right to decline or terminate any engagement where due diligence is incomplete, where information provided is inaccurate, or where we reasonably suspect money laundering, terrorist financing, tax evasion or other illegal activity. Where the law requires us to report a suspicion, we may be prohibited from telling you that we have done so.
