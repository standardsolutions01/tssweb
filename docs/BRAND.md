## ⚠️ Compliance — must follow
| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| “Chartered”, “ACCA”, “ICAEW”, “CIMA” (about TSS or the founder) | “ACMA (ICMA Pakistan)”, “qualified management accountant” |
| “We are your US registered agent” | “Registered-agent service provided via our partner” |
| “Australian tax agent / BAS agent” | “Bookkeeping and advisory in Australia” |
| “We register your UK company at Companies House” (as if TSS files directly) | “Filed with/through our authorised formation partners” |
| Invented reviews, client logos, statistics | Only real, approved testimonials |
| Exact government fees | “Check current fees on the official site” |
| Legal advice | “We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice.” |

Pricing is **never published** — always “Contact for pricing”. Payment terms for setup services: 60% advance, 40% on completion.
